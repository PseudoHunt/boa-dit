## Findings

Percentages are reductions in rel. latent MSE (positive = better, negative = worse), with paired bootstrap 95% CIs over the 512 images.

- Reweighting calibration statistics by sampler gain and adaLN gate energy does not help. `full` vs `uniform`: W4 -11.7% [-17.0, -6.9], W3 +6.5% [+5.1, +8.0] (Gate 2 STOP). The pre-registered W4 diagnostic set (`gate2b.md`) found no arm ≥ 5% (STOP).
- Time-indexed low-rank residual correction loses to a static one at equal extra bits: time2-16 vs static-32 W4 -6.6% [-9.3, -4.0], W3 -19.1% [-21.9, -16.6] (Gate A STOP). The static correction itself is effective: static-32 vs uniform W4 +28.4% [+25.4, +31.5], W3 +68.3% [+66.9, +69.7], for 0.593 extra bits/param (not FID-checked).
- Error concentrates in one place: swapping block 2 back to FP removes 23% of the W4 error, 21% from its `attn.qkv` alone.
- Keeping the top-100 rows at W8 RTN increases error (gate-ranked W4 -4.5% [-5.3, -3.7], error-ranked W4 -4.5% [-5.4, -3.7]; W3 -8.8% [-9.1, -8.5] / -8.5% [-8.8, -8.2]). **This is unexplained**: higher precision cannot raise a layer's own error. Suspects: inter-layer cancellation of quantization errors, or an interaction between the guard and BoA's sequential calibration (later blocks were calibrated on inputs from the un-guarded model). Not investigated further; no further runs on this project.
- Infrastructure: GPU peer-to-peer copies on the host were silently wrong. One eval was invalidated, quarantined (`stage2/eval_invalid/`) and rerun; all checkpoint loads now go through CPU and are verified.
