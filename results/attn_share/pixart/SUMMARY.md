## What it means

- **Prompt-independence survives text conditioning.** Early self-attention rows agree across different prompts as much as across seeds of one prompt (median ratio 1.000). The cross-attention control (block 13, heads [0, 7, 15]) is strongly prompt-dependent (diff/same ratio 0.08–0.11 at s ≤ 7). See `cross_attention_control.png`.
- **But the shared window is short.** Cross-image agreement decays after step 2 of 20 (15% of the schedule), against 14 of 50 (30%) on class-conditional DiT-XL/2 at 256 px. That fails A as pre-registered. The pre-registered STOP label ("specific to class-conditional DiT") overstates it: the image-independence is real but brief, while prompt-independence holds in 90% of (block, head, step ≤ 7) triples.
- **The linear-time form is ruled out.** With the floor removed (min κ, 5% of the mass), the residual 95% support is the full 127 × 127 offset range at the median (62% of kernels exactly full). With the median floor (44% of the mass) it is still 127. "Global mean of V + small convolution" does not describe these maps, matching DiT-XL/2 at 256 px.
