# G3 — does quantization sensitivity rank differently by quality than by identity? (pre-registration)

Written 2026-10-25, while G2 was running and before any G3 run. G3 runs only if G2 passes. Results are appended below
the marker by `scripts/make_tsens_g3.py`; nothing above the marker changes afterwards.

## Setup
Three model instances on one GPU: FP, uniform W4 and uniform W3 (`uniform_w{4,3}_K1_lam1_brcfg`, loaded via CPU with the
tensor check). A routing wrapper sends each sampler step to one of them. DDIM-50, cfg 1.5, bs 32, the standard seed
layout. Buckets: B0 = steps 0–9, B1 = 10–19, B2 = 20–29, B3 = 30–39, B4 = 40–49 (0 = noisiest).

## Arms
- **P0–P4:** W3 inside bucket b, FP elsewhere.
- **I0:** W3 in B0, W4 elsewhere. **I4:** W3 in B4, W4 elsewhere.
- **Noise:** FP on seeds 2000–3999 (FID-2k). The earlier `fp` check arm reused seeds 0–1999, so it gives no seed-to-seed
  spread. spread = |FID-2k(FP, 2000–3999) − FID-2k(FP, 0–1999 anchor 23.89)|.

## Cached once
The FP trajectory for seeds 0–511 at every step (x before each of the 50 steps, plus the final), fp16.

## Metrics per arm
One 2,000-image generation per arm on seeds 0–1999. Its first 512 images are the latent-MSE seeds, in the same batch
layout.
1. **Identity:** final-latent rel-MSE vs FP, seeds 0–511.
2. **Quality:** FID-2k, seeds 0–1999.
3. **Labels:** ResNet-50 top-1 label accuracy and the changed-class fraction vs FP, seeds 0–511.
4. **Per-step deviation:** rel-MSE against the cached FP latent at every step, seeds 0–511.
5. **Late-anchored relMSE:** both FP and the arm restart from the cached FP latent at step 25 and run steps 25–49; the
   rel-MSE is taken between the two finals. For P0 and P1 this is exactly 0, the sanity check.

## Verdict (handoff §3)
- s_id(b) = identity relMSE of P_b. s_q(b) = FID-2k(P_b) − 23.89.
- **PASS** iff argmax_b s_q ≠ argmax_b s_id **and** Spearman(s_q, s_id) over the five buckets is ≤ 0.5.
- Spread rule: the two argmaxes count as different only if s_q at the quality argmax exceeds s_q at the identity
  argmax by more than 2 × spread. Otherwise they are treated as equal.
- **STOP** otherwise: G4 is skipped, and the write-up is parts 1 + 2 only.

Recorded either way:
- whether label accuracy is flat across buckets;
- whether the per-step deviation curves grow, hold or shrink after the perturbed bucket ends.
