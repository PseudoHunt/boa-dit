## Reading (written after confirmation; the pre-registered rule decided)
- **Primary (CBλ / C_last49): useful** — 0.292 [0.174, 0.440], 62/64 images better, ΔLPIPS −0.051 [−0.067, −0.036]. The unscaled bridge is better still:
  CB1 / C 0.213 [0.130, 0.335], 61/64, ΔLPIPS −0.055, at the same 50 cheap / 13 FP calls (zero extra calls; one held latent-sized tensor).
- **The gain is not the final-interval gap:** moving the last anchor to step 49 is slightly worse than C (1.005 [1.004, 1.007]; pre-registered reading "harmful").
- **Network response within a window is small here:** the coefficient-only hold residual matches the FP-replayed window deviation (cos 0.994, magnitude 1.08).
  The bridge's limitation is the linear-interpolation estimate of the path (cos 0.68 with the actual deviation, 44% of its energy removed), not the omitted
  Jacobian. Consecutive anchor jumps are anti-correlated (cos −0.24), so forward extrapolation is not indicated.
- **Re-evaluating at the corrected state did not add a measurable benefit** (CB1 / CB1_re 0.941 [0.588, 1.470]); the zero-call approximation was adequate
  in this setting, though the first-order mismatch was not shown to be negligible in general.
- **The conservative multiplier was not better:** CBλ / CB1 1.380 [0.994, 1.942]. λ* was fit to the local window deviation; the endpoint benefited from
  the full correction. The unscaled formula was the pre-registered control and is the better configuration on these data.
- Scope: one cheap model (static A8, feedback off), DiT-XL/2 256, K = 4, fake-quant fidelity to FP-50; dev seeds were inspected; 64 fresh images; no
  real-kernel timing or FID. Not tested: activation feedback with the bridge (AC + bridge), other K, W8 anchors, other models. Each needs its own registration.
