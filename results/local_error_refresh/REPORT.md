## Reading (written after E1; the protocol stop rule decided, not this text)
- **Fresh oracle weak at the tested sites → the joint three-site pilot stopped at the E1 headroom rule.** Refreshing the local weight correction at every step
  (K = 1, the most favourable case) at all three selected sites did not lower endpoint error: O3/G 1.018 [0.991, 1.055], OA3/A 1.005 [0.965, 1.063]; single sites
  0.997–1.005. H1 (held corrections useful) and H2 (feedback adds value to them) were therefore **not tested**; no held-correction, mixed-precision, anchoring
  or cost comparison was made. This is not a claim that no local correction can work: other sites, more sites, or other models were not screened.
- Persistence was not the obstacle. eW_t = (M − W) h_t is strongly persistent (uncentered correlation 0.98 at lag 1, 0.89 at lag 4; a K4 schedule would leave
  5.7% of its energy, K8 17.7%), and feedback barely changes it (A: 5.9% / 18.0%). The limit is headroom: a fresh correction at the best single site removed only
  ≈ 1.2% of G's same-state prediction SSE (E0), and three sites' sum did not reach the endpoint.
- Incidental (dev only, exploratory): with clipping endpoints matched, temporal feedback state helps — A/G 0.886 [0.839, 0.932], 15/16 wins, ΔLPIPS −0.021;
  the guarded quantizer's range handling alone gives G/Q 0.977 [0.958, 0.993].
