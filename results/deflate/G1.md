# Deflate-and-carry — G1: does the token-mean direction dominate, and is it the adaLN shift? (pre-registration)

Written 2026-10-25, before any G1 statistic was computed. Results are appended below the marker by
`scripts/make_deflate_g1.py`; nothing above the marker changes afterwards.

## Data
FP DiT-XL/2 256, cfg 1.5, DDIM-50, calibration seeds 1000–1031, both CFG branches, all 50 steps, all 28 blocks × 4
linears (`attn.qkv`, `attn.proj`, `mlp.fc1`, `mlp.fc2`). Inputs propagate through **FP** blocks. G1 is FP-only, as
specified; G2's quantization recomputes everything on its own quantized-prefix inputs.

Per-sample weights `w_s` are exactly those of the uniform arm (`weights.combine`, branch cfg, K = 1, λ = 1): each
(bucket, branch) mean Gram E[x xᵀ] is weighted by 1/B × the normalized branch weight s² : (s − 1)² = 2.25 : 0.25. So:
- H = Σ_{b,br} w_{b,br} · (1/n_{b,br}) Σ_i x_i x_iᵀ
- H_mean = Σ_{b,br} w_{b,br} · (1/n_{b,br}) Σ_s 256 · x̄_s x̄_sᵀ
- H_var = H − H_mean (exact)

## Statistics
As in the handoff, §1. Choices fixed here:
- **Token means:** x̄_s is not written to disk. Its reductions (H_mean, per-bucket traces, the cosines to the predicted
  p_s, and Σ p_s p_sᵀ for u_1) are accumulated on the fly from the same pass. This is mathematically identical, and
  it saves about 2.5 GB.
- **Eigendecompositions:** fp64, on the GPU with a CPU fallback.
- **r_ℓ:** the smallest r with the top-r eigenvalues of H_mean ≥ 95% of tr(H_mean), capped at 8.
- **r\*:** the median over the 112 layers of r_ℓ (lower median if the count is even), at least 1.
- **Erased directions:** #{eig(H) < 0.01 · mean diag H} vs #{eig(H_P) < 0.01 · mean diag H_P} − r_ℓ. The r_ℓ
  directions projected out of H_P are carried exactly, not erased, so they are subtracted.
- **Mechanism, p_s per sample from the block's own adaLN modulation:**
  - qkv: shift_msa
  - fc1: shift_mlp
  - proj: W_v · shift_msa + b_v (the V slice of qkv)
  - fc2: GELU_tanh(W_1 · shift_mlp + b_1)

  Reported: the median over samples of cos(x̄_s, p_s), and |cos(v_1, u_1)|, where u_1 is the top eigenvector of
  Σ_s p_s p_sᵀ (unweighted). That eigenvector equals the top left-singular vector of the stacked p_s. The sign is
  arbitrary, so the absolute value is taken.

## Verdict (handoff §1)
- **STOP** if the mean-energy share tr(H_mean) / tr(H) is below 0.3 in more than 80% of the 112 layers.
- Otherwise G1 passes and outputs `configs/deflate_r.json` (r_ℓ per layer, r\*).
- **Mechanism PASS** (a finding, not a stop rule): the median cos(x̄_s, p_s) is ≥ 0.9 in the layers whose
  mean-energy share is ≥ 0.5. It is evaluated as ≥ 0.9 in every such layer, and the fraction of such layers that
  clear 0.9 is reported too.
