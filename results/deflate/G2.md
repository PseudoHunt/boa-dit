# Deflate-and-carry — G2: does deflate-and-carry beat uniform BoA? (pre-registration)

Written 2026-10-25, after G1 (PASS, r\* = 8, mechanism FAIL) and before any G2 quantization. Results are appended below
the marker by `scripts/make_deflate_g2.py`; nothing above the marker changes afterwards.

## Configuration (identical to the uniform arm except for the Hessian and the carry)
BoA fork GPTQ path, per-channel asymmetric, Hessian grid search, K = 1, λ = 1, branch cfg, `act_order` off, no
dead-row rule (every row goes through the solver), adaLN W8 RTN, sequential block-by-block calibration on
quantized-prefix inputs, 32 calibration images × 2 branches × 50 steps. The solver code is unchanged; it is only
called with a different H.

## Arms
Seven quantizations. r\* = 8 > 1, so DC-1 also runs at W4.

| arm | Hessian given to the solver | carry directions V | bits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DC-r\* | P H P, r = r_ℓ per layer (`configs/deflate_r.json`) | top-r_ℓ eigenvectors of H_mean | W4, W3 |
| DC-1 | P H P, r = 1 | top-1 eigenvector of H_mean | W4, W3 |
| C-r\* | H (as uniform) | the same V as DC-r\*, loaded from DC-r\*'s `carry.pt` | W4, W3 |
| WSVD-r\* | P H P with P from V | top-r_ℓ right singular vectors of the FP weight W | W3 |

- **H_mean** is formed at quantization time from the same quantized-prefix calibration pass, with the same weights as
  H (G1's definition). The **ranks** r_ℓ come from G1, which used FP-propagated inputs.
- **Carry:** δ = (W − W_q) V, computed in fp32 and stored in fp16 with V. y = W_q x + b + δ (Vᵀ x). It is installed
  as soon as a layer is quantized. Later layers in the same block keep the Grams collected before any layer of the
  block was quantized, exactly as the uniform arm does. The next block's inputs are produced with the carry active.
- **Reuse, not rerun:** uniform W4 / W3 (latent MSE and FID-2k 29.11 / 130.91; FP 23.89), and Gate A static-32
  (latent MSE).
- **Evaluation:** latent MSE on seeds 0–511 for all seven. FID-2k on seeds 0–1999 for DC-r\* W4 and W3, C-r\* W3,
  WSVD-r\* W3, and DC-1 W3. A matched-seed grid at W3.
- **Diagnostics per arm:**
  - extra bits per parameter (fp16 V and δ), per layer type and for the whole model;
  - ‖W_q − W‖_F / ‖W‖_F per layer, against the uniform arm (the drift check);
  - clipping fraction per layer: the share of FP weights outside the row's quantized range [min W_q, max W_q].
- **Drift rule:** if any layer's drift exceeds 3× the uniform arm's at a bit-width, one extra arm runs at that
  bit-width with damping 0.05 · mean diag(H_P). The solver adds 0.01 · mean diag itself, so (0.04 / 1.01) · mean
  diag(H_P) is pre-added to H_P. Both are reported.

## Verdict (handoff §2)
**PASS** by either route:
- **Route A:** FID-2k(DC-r\*, W3) ≤ 45.
- **Route B:** FID-2k(DC-r\*, W4) ≤ 28.5 **and** relMSE(DC-r\*, W4) ≤ 0.90 × relMSE(uniform, W4).

**STOP** otherwise: G3 and G4 are not run.

Findings (not stop rules):
- **Deflation earns its place** if relMSE(DC-r\*) ≤ 0.95 × relMSE(C-r\*) at both bit-widths; otherwise the method
  is the carry alone.
- **Direction matters** if DC-r\* beats WSVD-r\* at W3 on both latent MSE and FID-2k.
- **r = 1 suffices** if DC-1 is within 5% of DC-r\* on latent MSE at W3.

### Matched-bits reading

- W4: DC-8 at 4.14 bits/weight: latent MSE 0.07265; Gate A static-32 at its own 4.59 bits/weight: 0.06506 (not matched; static-32 spends 4.2× the extra bits).
- W3: DC-8 at 3.14 bits/weight: latent MSE 0.16179; Gate A static-32 at its own 3.59 bits/weight: 0.12509 (not matched; static-32 spends 4.2× the extra bits).

### Verdict

- Route A: DC-r* W3 + carry, 3.14 bits/weight: FID-2k 65.19 (≤ 45: **False**).
- Route B: DC-r* W4 + carry, 4.14 bits/weight: FID-2k 27.91 (≤ 28.5: True) and relMSE ratio vs uniform 0.799 (≤ 0.90: True) → **True**.

**G2: PASS** via Route B (W4 + carry, 4.14 bits/weight, FID-2k 27.91).

Verdict written 2026-10-25 from the two DC-r* FIDs, the only inputs to the rule. The ablation and baseline rows were still running and do not enter it.
