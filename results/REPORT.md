## 2026-10-25 — Stage 1 (diagnostics) complete — Gate 1: PASS
- Ran (FP only, seeds 2000-2015): perturbation sweeps (fp16 at rho 1e-4/3e-3/1e-2/3e-2, fp32 at 1e-3/3e-3), gate
  energies, Gram traces, per-bucket Gram dissimilarity, token norms, attention-position stability (4.7).
  Full numbers: `results/gate1.md` (generated by `scripts/make_gate1.py`).
- GPU-hours: 1.20 (budget 1.5).
- Verdict numbers: gain2 max/median 17.0 (chosen estimator; >= 15 under every estimator and rho);
  gate_mlp criterion in 12/28 blocks (gate_msa 5/28); per-bucket Grams differ in 12/12 (block, layer) pairs
  (median rel. Frobenius 0.09-1.00, min Spearman 0.01-0.71). No token sinks (worst max/median 7.6) -> lambda = 1 only
  in Gate 2.
- **Deviation needing a human look — the gain estimator.** The handoff's ratio-of-means at rho = 0.01 is not
  rho-independent (rho 0.01 vs 0.03: 41/50 steps >10% apart). Two causes: (1) the fp16 sampler has a rounding-chaos
  floor (rho = 1e-4 reads an apparent gain2 of 10^1-10^3), so rho cannot be lowered in fp16 -> reran in fp32;
  (2) even in fp32, 1-2 of 16 seeds sit near a trajectory bifurcation and dominate the mean (their gain grows as rho
  shrinks). Median-over-seeds of the per-seed ratio, fp32, is rho-independent (1e-3 vs 3e-3: 0/50 steps >10% apart,
  median 0.1%) and is what Stage 2 uses (`configs/gain_choice.json`). Ratio-of-means stays a Stage 3 ablation.
- 4.7 (log-only): per-(position, bucket) attention pooling **not plausible** (0/0/3 qualifying steps in blocks
  0/13/27): across-image agreement is high at noisy steps but drops to 0.3-0.6 late; patterns 25 steps apart stay
  similar (far > 0.5) except block 27 heads 0/15.
- Also found: block 27's gate_mlp has 76.7% dead channels; the same residual channels (293, 295, 900) carry the
  largest gates in most blocks.
- Bug caught and fixed before any numbers were used: forward hooks run inside the fp16 autocast region, so the
  first Gram pass accumulated x^T x in fp16. All Gram hooks now disable autocast and assert fp32.
- Next decision needing a human: confirm the gain-estimator deviation (Stage 2 proceeds with it meanwhile).
