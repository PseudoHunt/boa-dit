## Reading (written after confirmation; the pre-registered rules decided)
- **Drift vs local objective under anchoring: inconclusive in both cells.** Feedback off: C.D / C.L 1.185 [0.973, 1.466] (point estimate worse; LPIPS
  +0.007 [+0.000, +0.015]). Feedback on: AC.D / AC.L 0.943 [0.701, 1.308]. No demonstrated advantage of the drift objective under these conditions.
- **The objective did what it optimizes, at a cost the anchored sampler did not repay.** On held-out own trajectories, window persistence rose
  (ρ 0.69 → 0.91) and drift energy fell ~6% (C.L 1226 → C.D 1154; AC.L 1312 → AC.D 1224), while per-step error roughly tripled and unanchored endpoints
  became ~10× worse (dev Q.D / Q.L 9.9). The loss is blind to window-constant error, so the branch traded absolute accuracy for persistence; a 6% drift
  reduction did not reach the endpoint, plausibly because larger absolute errors change the trajectory and the anchor-step behaviour (not established).
- **Feedback after calibration:** AC.D / C.D 1.076 [0.942, 1.237] and AC.L / C.L 1.353 [0.967, 1.928] — inconclusive; feedback has not been shown to help
  under anchoring with any branch tested (consistent with aa1).
- **Incidental:** the joint pilot's ordinary local refit improves the anchored sampler without feedback: C.L / C0 0.744 [0.545, 0.934], 47/64.
- Limitations: one training seed per arm, 200 updates, fixed u200, uniform weights, no anchor-energy regularizer (the natural next variant would add
  a small ‖e_t‖² term to remove the blind direction — not run, needs its own registration). 64 images.
